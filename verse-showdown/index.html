    How scoring works
    • Book: 250 points for the right book. A wrong book earns up to 125 if it is close in the Bible's order.
    • Chapter: up to 250 points when the book is right, fewer the further you are from the right chapter.
    • Verse: up to 250 points when the chapter is right, fewer the further you are from the right verse.
    • Words: up to 250 points for how many words of Bible text lie between your answer and the verse. The points halve every 5,000 words.
    • A perfect answer scores 1000.
